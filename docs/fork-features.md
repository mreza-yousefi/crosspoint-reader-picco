# Fork features (BOOX Picco)

What this fork adds on top of upstream CrossPoint, and where each is documented.

## Touch lock (slide switch set to Touch controls while reading)
Details: [USER_GUIDE.md section 3.6.3](../USER_GUIDE.md#363-controls).

| Feature | How |
|---|---|
| Full touch lock | Each switch move toggles it (position does not matter); all touch ignored, side buttons work; kept through sleep; a switch moved while asleep toggles it at wake |
| Lock from the light panel | Touch tile (pointer) toggles the lock; locking closes the panel |
| Lock indicator | Padlock centred in the top bar and in the top page margin (top-right corner when the margin is too small); open while overridden (from Home, or in a book while the toolbar opened with Up + Down has touch) |
| Light panel under the lock | Up + Down together (touch works inside the panel); in a book (EPUB, TXT, Markdown, Toolbar menu style) the reader toolbar opens at the bottom with it, so the menus can be reached: tap the Contents, Text or More icon to close the panel and open that item (history Back/Forward jump at once and close both), touch stays on until the page is back; closing the panel (Up + Down, Back, a tap on the page) closes both |
| Refresh under the lock | Power + Up together |
| Change book under the lock | Up + Down, then the house icon in the light panel goes to Home; pick a book with the buttons |
| Dictionary under the lock | Hold Up 0.4 s on a book page, tap a word: definition at once (no menu); Down backs out (also from "Not Found"); short Up turns back on release |
| Unlock from Home | Hold Up 0.4 s on Home; ends on opening a book, holding Down, moving the switch, sleep, 2 min idle |
| Wake status | Waking into a book: light panel with "Touch locked · light check - tap below to read"; waking to Home: "Touch locked" note |
| Shortcut safety | A shortcut's buttons never also turn a page; a page turned by a not-quite-simultaneous press is undone, before the light panel opens (the page under the panel is the one you were on); no sleep while a shortcut is held |

## Dictionary
Details: [docs/dictionary.md](dictionary.md).

- Definition sheet over the lower 60% of the screen, with the book page visible above; swipe or side buttons page, tap above closes.
- Dictionary-mode menu: Look Up / Another word / + (then Clip / Bookmark / <). Under the lock no menu: the word is looked up on release, and the definition offers Another word / + above the sheet.
- Less sensitive word selection in dictionary mode; returns to the book after a lookup.
- A word not found shows "Not Found" with **Another word** (back to word selection on the same page) and **Close** (back to the book); also Confirm / Back, and Down under the lock.
- Same large buttons in every selection menu; Look Up turns black with "Looking Up..." while it works.
- One clean refresh when the definition sheet appears, and one when "Not Found" closes.
- Mistaken lookups are not kept: a lookup is saved to the vocabulary history and counted in the reading stats only once its definition has been on screen for 1 s (`LOOKUP_MIN_VISIBLE_MS`). Words not found are never recorded.
- The light panel can open over the definition sheet; closing it puts back the page above the sheet.
- Lookup history: upstream Vocabulary History (`/Vocabulary.txt`), on by default in this fork (replaces the earlier `/vocabulary/lookups.tsv`).
- Persian definitions: add Persian glyphs to your reading font with `scripts/merge_persian_font.py`.

## Home
Details: [USER_GUIDE.md section 3.1](../USER_GUIDE.md#31-home-screen).

- Hold a recent book on Home (touch long-press on the cover or Continue Reading row, or a 1 s Confirm hold where a Confirm button exists; under the touch lock hold Up on Home first): menu with the book title, **Open** and **Remove from Recents**. Remove asks to confirm, then Home reloads without the book. Only the Recents entry is removed; the book file, progress, bookmarks and per-book settings stay. Home shows only books from the Recents list, newest first, as many as fit (the first books of the Library's Recent tab); in the Cover Grid, empty slots stay empty instead of being filled from the Library. A removed book stays off Home until it is opened again. Upstream **Hide New Files in Recent** (Settings → Library) does not change Home; it makes the Library's Recent tab list only opened books, the same books Home shows.
- EPUB cover thumbnails that cannot be made are remembered, so Home (standard and Cover Grid) no longer shows the loading bar on every visit. An empty thumbnail file for that size in the book cache marks the cover, as upstream already did for unsupported image formats, and Home draws the placeholder. Only clear cases are marked: the book names no cover (and its guide cover page, if any, was read), the cover file is missing from the book, the image format is not supported, or the decoder failed with enough free memory and the whole cover extracted. Everything else (low memory, SD errors, other extraction errors, GIF covers) is tried again on the next visit. The serial log has one line per failed attempt: `cover thumb: marked (...)` or `cover thumb: retry later (...)`. To try a marked cover again, open the book and use **Delete Book Cache** in the reader menu (this book only; reading progress is kept), or **Clear Reading Cache** (all books; reading progress is lost). Replacing the book over Wi-Fi also clears it. A different thumbnail size (other theme) tries again too.
- A touch hold on a menu row or Cover Grid tab still opens it (when the hold is detected, not on release); a Confirm hold on a menu item opens it on release, as before. A hold on empty space does nothing; a hold on the status bar opens the light panel, like a tap.

## Light
- Light panel: a Home tile replaces the Bluetooth tile (Bluetooth stays in Settings). Under the touch lock, open the panel with Up + Down and tap Home to change books.
- Warmth reads Off, 1-99%, Full (Off = cool light only, Full = warm light only). On the BOOX Picco there is no colour jump inside 1-99% at any brightness; the only visible step is into Off or Full. Fix merged upstream: Free-Ink/freeink-sdk#172.
- Smooth brightness and warmth steps using all ten vendor levels (`freeink-sdk` branch `picco-custom`; reported as Free-Ink/freeink-sdk#167, since fixed in the upstream SDK as well).
- Brightness/warmth greyed out while the light is off; lamp button reliably turns it on.
- Holding - or + in the light panel keeps stepping (first repeat after 0.4 s, then every 0.15 s); the settings are saved once, when the panel closes.
- Wake with light: hold Down while pressing Power to wake; the light comes on at the saved brightness/warmth, with no screenshot or page turn; the light panel opens with a note so the light can be adjusted (not when the slide switch is set to Frontlight). The light is saved as on, so the next wake with Restore Light on Wake on keeps it on.

## Per-book text settings
Details: [USER_GUIDE.md section 3.6.2](../USER_GUIDE.md#362-reader).

- Each EPUB book keeps its own Text Settings (font, size, spacing, indent, alignment, margin, style toggles) in `/.crosspoint/book-settings/<KOReader document id>.json`; the id hashes the book's content, so renaming or moving the book keeps them, and the book file is never changed.
- Settings → Reader → Text Settings (and the web settings page) hold the defaults; a new book starts from them. Changes inside a book never change them. While a book is open SETTINGS holds the book's values and `CrossPointSettings` keeps the defaults aside: `toJson()` writes the defaults for the per-book keys, so `settings.json` never holds a book's values (also on a save, sleep or power loss mid-read), and leaving the reader puts the defaults back. A book without a document id keeps in-book changes until it is closed.
- Leaving that global Text Settings after a change asks "Apply changes to": New books only (also Back) or All books. All books writes only the keys changed in this visit into every file in `/.crosspoint/book-settings/`, one file at a time; each book keeps its other values. A family change sends the size only if a size was also chosen (books snap their size on open). Unreadable files are skipped and logged, then "Some books not updated" shows. The section cache header holds the layout values, so a book whose layout value changed is laid out again when next opened; anti-aliasing applies when drawing. Home or sleep from this screen, and the web settings page, change the new-book values only.
- The reader toolbar's Text panel has a Text Anti-Aliasing row (tap toggles, saved to the book). Turning AA off makes the next page B/W only and forces one HALF refresh, since a fast refresh would leave the gray AA pixels on the glass.
- Text Settings → Layout → Reset Text Settings (book open only) restores the factory values for this book.
- Clear Reading Cache does not delete them; Home Backup includes them.

## Display
- Fast Page Turns (Settings → Display, BOOX Picco with the SSD2677 panel only; default On): keeps the panel powered between refreshes, so a page turn skips the power-on and power-off waits. Off powers the panel down after every refresh. (The upstream SDK decides by itself: it powers the panel down after book pages and full-screen redraws, and keeps it on after small updates such as a moved selection.) Whatever the setting, the panel is powered down before sleep (from the start of the sleep sequence, so the sleep screen and the USB-powered sleep wait run with it off). Switching the setting off powers the panel down at once. Needs a fork change in `freeink-sdk` (branch `picco-custom`): the SSD2677 driver follows the caller's turn-off flag instead of its own rule (first a revert of upstream commit 0ca892a; the upstream waveform choices, night-mode and grayscale fixes are kept). The same rule covers the one-pass anti-aliased text refresh for book pages (upstream Free-Ink/freeink-sdk#213) and the clean refresh before it; upstream always powers off after the text refresh. A text pass that is cancelled still shows the page, and that refresh always powers off. The boot log shows the mode (`Panel power between refreshes`).

## Reader
- Battery and clock in the top page margin (EPUB, TXT, Markdown), as in the Home header: clock top-left (or left of the battery when set to Right), battery and percentage top-right, padlock centred. Only when the screen margin is larger than the small font's line height; otherwise they stay in the bottom status bar. XTC unchanged.
- Fast page-turn presses (EPUB, TXT, Markdown) are queued while a page draws (up to 20) and each page is drawn in turn; a press the other way cancels one queued turn. Bluetooth remote turns queue behind them. Opening a menu, panel or tool, adding a bookmark, or jumping (link, footnote, chapter) drops the queue.
- Book position (EPUB, TXT, Markdown) in paper pages, `p. page/total`, in the right corner of the status bar; chapter page and percentage moved to the left corner, before battery and clock (also in XTC books). A paper page is 1,800 characters of the book's text (like a typical paperback; may differ from a specific printed edition), whitespace runs counted once, so the number does not depend on font, size, margins or orientation. The text of each spine item is counted once per book in idle loop frames, one item per frame, and saved as `paper_pages.bin` in the book cache after each item, so a count stopped by sleep or closing the book goes on from there. A button press, or a touch on the BOOX Picco, stops the item being counted at once; it starts over in a later idle frame. Counting pauses while a menu or panel is open, while the Bluetooth page turner holds its heap, and while a chapter builds. The position appears a few seconds after the first time it can show in a book. The current page uses the page's exact text offset in the chapter. Shown with Book Progress Percentage on; see [USER_GUIDE.md section 3.6.1](../USER_GUIDE.md#361-display).
- Space Above Status Bar / Space Below Clock/Battery (Customise Status Bar; None/Small/Medium/Large = 0/8/16/32 px; defaults Small/None) add space between the page text and the bottom status bar, and between the top-margin battery/clock line and the text (EPUB, TXT, Markdown). The bottom space applies when a line is drawn at the bottom (an item that stays in the bottom bar, or the automatic page turn line); the top space when battery or clock is shown in the top margin. The page viewport is smaller, so section caches rebuild (viewport height is in the section header): after updating, each book lays its chapters out once again on first open. Paper pages are unaffected. The space is added to the upstream spacing: the bottom of the text keeps the screen margin plus the status bar height (upstream changed this from the larger of the two).
- Status-bar clock (EPUB, TXT, Markdown) repaints at each new minute with one fast refresh: no gray pass, no step towards the periodic clean refresh. Not during automatic page turn, with a menu, panel, popup or message over the page, or on UC8279 absolute-gray image pages ([USER_GUIDE.md section 3.6.1](../USER_GUIDE.md#361-display)).
- Focus Reading leaves Arabic-script words (Persian, Arabic, Urdu) without the bold start, which would break the joining of their letters; other words keep it (book pages and the definition sheet). The section cache version changed, so each book lays its chapters out once again on first open.
- Jump history (EPUB): **Back** and **Forward** in the reader menu, shown only when there is a target. List menu: below Select Chapter, with the target's book percent as the value. Toolbar: arrow buttons at the ends of the tool row (Back left of Contents, Forward right of More; not in the More panel), one tap jumps and closes the toolbar, also when the toolbar opens with Up+Down under the touch lock; Left/Right reach them with the buttons. The row always has five equal slots (an unavailable button and the panels' switcher leave the ends empty), so the tool icons never move; focus on a button that disappears (after a chapter skip) moves to the nearest tool. Jumps are recorded before they move: contents (list and toolbar), links and footnotes, Go to %, bookmarks, clippings, chapter skips (long press, remote, toolbar arrows and scrub) and a KOReader Sync download. Page turns are not. A run of chapter skips counts once (only the first records); a page turn, any other jump or closing the toolbar ends the run. Browser model: Back swaps the current position in for the target, so Forward returns where Back was pressed; a new jump drops the Forward entries; leaving the place the newest Back entry already holds adds nothing. Last 50 entries per book in `history.bin` in the book cache (4-byte header, 12 bytes per entry: spine, page, page count, visible text offset, percent, flags), written on every change. The visible text offset finds the page again after re-layout. Without an offset, the page keeps its relative place in the chapter (page / old page count, applied to the new page count). The menu entries are hidden on the end-of-book screen; the Back button still returns from a link there, and Forward then comes back to the last chapter's last page. A bookmark or clipping on the page already shown records nothing. This replaces the link back-stack (3 entries, page numbers, `links.bin`): an entry left by a followed link is marked, and the Back button (and the Footnotes shortcuts) return while the newest Back entry is such a link. Upstream kept the link positions even after another jump. A `links.bin` from older firmware is imported as link entries when the history is empty, then removed. Not in XTC books.
- The RTC is set at each SNTP sync and from the Home Backup server's `Date` header after a run that ends Ok or Partial.

## Reading statistics
Details: [docs/reading-stats.md](reading-stats.md) (data, rules, formats) and [USER_GUIDE.md section 3.9](../USER_GUIDE.md#39-reading-stats-fork).

- Every EPUB, TXT and Markdown reading session and every page change is recorded on the SD card in `/.crosspoint/stats/` (XTC is not). A book is recognised by its KOReader document id, so renaming or moving it keeps its statistics.
- Active time: a page counts up to about 3.5x the time you usually need for that much text in this book (at least 1 minute, at most 30); longer is a pause. The speed is learned per book; before that, from all books, else a flat 5 minutes.
- A session ends when the book closes, you go Home or sleep. Sessions of one book less than 10 minutes apart are one sitting. Time with the reader menu or the Stats screen over the book is not counted; dictionary lookups are, kept apart as dictionary time (no page cap, no speed sample; at most 10 minutes per definition). A session ended by automatic sleep counts its last page (or open definition) at most as one typical page: own speed, else all books' speed, else 1 minute; not when it slept with the menu open. **Ignore Short Opens in Stats** (Settings → Reader; also in the Stats Overview; default On) leaves out sessions under 1 minute or without a page turn.
- Reading speed comes from the exact text of pages turned forward in a chapter, so it does not need the status-bar page count. A book is finished when a session takes it past 99% (the end-of-book screen counts as 100%).
- Without a trusted clock, reading counts in Total but not in days or charts.
- **Reading Stats** screen: Overview (today, this week, month and year, total, current and longest streak, books finished this year) and bar charts of reading time per day (30), week (26), month (12) and year. Tap a bar or walk the bars with the buttons; the arrows, a swipe, or walking past the oldest bar pages back in time. A day bar opens that day's sessions; a week or month bar opens its reading days.
- **Books** and **Hidden Books** lists (last read first, with time and reading style) and a book page: Summary (time, sessions, sittings, average session, first and last read, reading style, speed in paper pages per hour, time to finish or finish date, progress, lookups, dictionary time, Hide from Stats switch, Compare with Another Book), Progress (position per reading day) and Sessions (newest 100). Reading styles: Stalled, Binge, Snacking, Steady, Intermittent (rules in docs/reading-stats.md).
- **When I Read**: weekday x hour grid shaded by reading time over the last 30 days, 12 months or all time.
- **What Hooked Me** (from a book's page): chapters from the book's raw log: time, speed, session starts and stops, "one more chapter" sessions, re-read text, turns back, pauses and lookups per chapter, with Highlights and chapter names from the table of contents when the book's cache is there.
- **Records** (longest session and sitting, most in a day and week, fastest book, longest streak, most lookups in a session, latest night, earliest morning), **Year in Review** (per year: time, sessions, books finished, top books, busiest month, weekday and hour, streak, lookups, late nights, styles) and **Reading Speed Trends** (by month, time of day, font size), from the Overview. Lookups per hour and late nights (23:00-04:00) per book and overall.
- Open it from Home (bar chart icon in the row of icons at the bottom of Home in the Cover Grid theme, **Reading Stats** in the Home list of the other themes) or from the reader menu (**Reading Stats**). Back goes to Home or to the book it was opened from.
- Hold a book on Home: **Hide from Stats** / **Show in Stats** keeps a test book out of the totals and charts; its data stays. **Reading Stats** in the same menu (Home, Library Recent view) and **Reading Stats for This Book** in the reader menu open the book page.
- Page data is kept in RAM and written every 3 minutes and at the session end, never on the page-turn path; a reset loses at most the last 3 minutes. Clear Reading Cache does not delete the statistics; Home Backup includes them.
- File dates on the SD card now come from the reader's clock (when it is set), so Home Backup sees files changed in place.
- **Web page and CSV export**: while File Transfer is on, `/stats` (link **Reading Stats** in the web header) shows the Overview, larger Days/Weeks/Months/Years charts, the weekday x hour grid, the Books list (hidden books marked) with each book's summary, progress, all sessions and chapters, Records, Year in Review and Speed, from JSON endpoints under `/api/stats/`. **Export** downloads `reading-sessions.csv` and `reading-books.csv`. Replies are streamed from the stats files; the page is static, served from flash, and uses no external resources. Endpoints: [webserver-endpoints.md](webserver-endpoints.md#reading-stats-api).

## Sleep screen
- Strip with "Battery at sleep N%" and "· Book N%". On USB power the first part reads "Charging N%" or "Charged N%".
- SSD2677 panel only (the one with Fast Page Turns): the strip is drawn over the gray sleep image with the stock mode-3 waveform, which leaves every pixel outside the strip undriven, so the image keeps its gray levels and there is no full-screen flash (`displayRetainedGrayscaleOverlay` in the `freeink-sdk` SSD2677 driver, branch `picco-custom`, after the overlay gray pass the Picco sleep screen uses; upstream implements it for SSD1677 absolute images only). Gray pixels inside the strip are driven to black or white; later updates change only the pixels that differ. The panel powers off after each update, as for the whole sleep sequence. Other panels, or no gray image on the glass: a fast refresh, as before.

## Home Backup
Details: [USER_GUIDE.md section 3.6.8](../USER_GUIDE.md#368-home-backup-fork) and [docs/home-backup.md](home-backup.md).

- Incremental backup to a WebDAV server (on request, or once a day at sleep; up to 60 s on battery, 15 min on USB power), fetches books from `/to-reader/`.
- No server details compiled in: enter URL and login on the reader; for HTTPS put the server certificate at `/.crosspoint/backup-ca.pem`.
